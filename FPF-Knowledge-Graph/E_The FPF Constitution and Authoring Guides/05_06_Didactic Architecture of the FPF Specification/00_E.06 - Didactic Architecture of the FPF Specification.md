## [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/05_06_Didactic Architecture of the FPF Specification/00_E.06 - Didactic Architecture of the FPF Specification|E.6]]:1 - Problem frame
FPF addresses readers who differ in at least two respects:

* **Disciplinary** – systems engineers, knowledge scientists, ethicists.
* **Experience** – newcomers need intuition; experts need rigour.

Past drafts mixed governance mandates with domain examples, producing a
steep learning curve and repeated “forward‑reference” detours.

## [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/05_06_Didactic Architecture of the FPF Specification/00_E.06 - Didactic Architecture of the FPF Specification|E.6]]:2 - Problem
If core ideas are buried under formalism or scattered across parts,
readers either give up or misuse the framework. We need a didactic
macro-order that guides cognitive load from low to high while keeping
normative sections discoverable, without letting readers confuse
document order with one universal first-practical workflow.

## [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/05_06_Didactic Architecture of the FPF Specification/00_E.06 - Didactic Architecture of the FPF Specification|E.6]]:4 - Solution — Order the explanation and keep practical entry separate

### [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/05_06_Didactic Architecture of the FPF Specification/00_E.06 - Didactic Architecture of the FPF Specification|E.6]]:4.0 - Document order is distinct from first-practical entry

The macro-order of the document is a didactic scaffold, not a universal practical workflow. Apply [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/10_11_First-Practical Entry and Pattern-Use Discoverability Discipline/03_E.11.PFP - Framework Publication Form Profile for Markdown|E.11.PFP]] to the compact opening and navigation. After the Table of Contents, the Readme offers recognizable working questions and direct entries; the Architectural Reference explains the whole pattern language, its cross-cutting ideas, connections and architectural choices, including an informal on-ramp. These informative explanation and navigation units and ToC cues may cross Parts when the reader's question requires it. Use [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/10_11_First-Practical Entry and Pattern-Use Discoverability Discipline/00_E.11 - First-Practical Entry and Pattern-Use Discoverability Discipline|E.11]] for practical entry, including its worked comparisons when compact cues leave a genuine ambiguity.

Keep the main explanatory sequence recoverable:

1. **Readme and Architectural Reference.** The Readme helps a reader find a first useful result. The Architectural Reference explains the connected language and its rationale; its introductory System and Episteme stories also build intuition before the kernel exposition.
2. **Part A — Kernel Architecture.** Introduce the holonic ontology and acting-side externalization in [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/12_Acting-Side Externalization and Reflexive Split- Identify Who Acts on What/00_A.12 - Acting-Side Externalization and Reflexive Split- Identify Who Acts on What|A.12]].
3. **Part B — Trans-disciplinary Reasoning.** Connect the general reasoning moves with applicable System and Episteme grounding under [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/06_07_Archetypal Grounding- Explain Rules of FPF Architectural Patterns through Cases/00_E.07 - Archetypal Grounding- Explain Rules of FPF Architectural Patterns through Cases|E.7]].
4. **Part C — Kernel Extension Specifications.** Develop the calculi and extension patterns whose questions use those foundations.
5. **Part D — Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization.** Introduce the reflective ethical and conflict questions with the needed holonic distinctions available.
6. **Part E — Constitution and Authoring Guides.** Place framework governance and contributor guidance after the principal kernel and reasoning exposition so an ordinary first use need not begin with authoring rules.
7. **Part F — Unification Suite.** Keep concept-set, sense and system-role unification methods in their own Part; reach them directly when the question calls for them.
8. **Part G — Discipline SoTA Patterns Kit.** Keep the discipline source-synthesis, comparison and refresh methods in their own Part.

Part H remains reserved. Examples and extended explanations belong with the pattern whose contribution they explain. Tooling guides and executable examples follow their separate family and lexical rules.

This publication order does not require readers to traverse every Part. Practical entries return to the pattern needed by the current question.

## [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/05_06_Didactic Architecture of the FPF Specification/00_E.06 - Didactic Architecture of the FPF Specification|E.6]]:8 - Rationale

The document order and [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/06_07_Archetypal Grounding- Explain Rules of FPF Architectural Patterns through Cases/00_E.07 - Archetypal Grounding- Explain Rules of FPF Architectural Patterns through Cases|E.7]] grounding rule provide those connections while leaving practical entries free to cross Parts.
