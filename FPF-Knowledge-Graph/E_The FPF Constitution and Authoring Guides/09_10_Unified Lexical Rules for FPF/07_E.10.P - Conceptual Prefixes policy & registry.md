> **Status:** Stable
 **Intent.** Provide a compact, **notation‑neutral** registry and **minting policy** for *conceptual prefixes* — short shorthands that signal **cognitive namespaces** used throughout the Core.

 **Policy (normative).**
1. **Purpose.** A conceptual prefix exists **to aid reasoning**, not to name files, serialisations, or APIs. It labels a **role in thought** (e.g., meta‑type, calculus operator, relation family).
 2. **Anchoring.** Every prefix **MUST** name the Core patterns that define its constructs, operators or vocabulary, or govern admission to its namespace. List these anchors in the registry and cite them in *Relations*. For a family with several definitions, name the supplying patterns for the relevant members.
 3. **No tool lock‑in.** A prefix **MUST NOT** imply a particular notation or machine binding (see [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/04_05_Four Guard-Rails of FPF/01_E.05.01 - DevOps Lexical Firewall|E.5.1]]–[[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/04_05_Four Guard-Rails of FPF/02_E.05.02 - Notational Independence|E.5.2]]).
 4. **Minting rule.** New prefixes are introduced by a **DRR** ([[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/08_09_Design-Rationale Record (DRR) for FPF Content Decisions/00_E.09 - Design-Rationale Record (DRR) for FPF Content Decisions|E.9]]) that demonstrates
    (a) cross‑pattern need,
    (b) non‑overlap with existing prefixes,
    (c) alignment with Pillars **P‑1/P‑5**.
 5. **Scope.** Prefixes are **globally reserved** within the Core; domain patterns  **MAY** mint local shorthands only inside their Contexts and **MUST NOT** collide with this registry.

 **Registered conceptual prefixes (Core).**
* `U.` — namespace for admitted U-kinds and dependent `U.*` forms; spelling alone does not prove kindhood. *Anchors:* [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/09_10_Unified Lexical Rules for FPF/00_E.10 - Unified Lexical Rules for FPF|E.10]]:8.3, M-P1, reserves the namespace; [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/23_24_U.Ontic and Ontic Introduction Discipline/03_E.24.UK - U-kind Admission and Ontic Settlement|E.24.UK]]:4.1 supplies the U-kind admission test. Each named value retains the identity or membership rule in its defining subject pattern.
* `Γ_` — **calculus-operator notation**, for example `Γ_epist`, `Γ_ctx` and `Γ_time`. *Anchors:* [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/09_10_Unified Lexical Rules for FPF/00_E.10 - Unified Lexical Rules for FPF|E.10]]:8.3, M-P1, reserves the prefix; [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/00_01_Holon Aggregation and Part-Whole Construction/03_B.01.03 - Knowledge Aggregation (Γepist)- Synthesis and Target-Scheme Compilation|B.1.3]]:4.2 defines the synthesis and compilation operators; [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/00_01_Holon Aggregation and Part-Whole Construction/04_B.01.04 - Specify Order-Sensitive or Temporal Aggregation (Γctx, Γtime)|B.1.4]]:3 defines the optional notation for contextual and temporal aggregation. Other flavours cite their own operator definitions. For a system aggregation or delimitation decision, use [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/00_01_Holon Aggregation and Part-Whole Construction/02_B.01.02 - Coordinate Decisions About System Aggregation and Delimitation|B.1.2]]:4 to recover the separately governed results and any missing basis.
* `ut:` — **Universal relation family** (e.g., `PartOf` sub‑relations). *Anchor:* [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/14_Advanced Mereology- Components, Portions, Aspects & Phases/00_A.14 - Advanced Mereology- Components, Portions, Aspects & Phases|A.14]] (Mereology) — informative alias vocabulary.
* `tv:` — **Trace & Validation vocabulary** (CT2R‑LOG): `tv:AliasOf`, `tv:groundedBy`. *Anchor:* [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/02_03_Is This Claim Supported for This Use- - Trust and Assurance Calculus/00_B.03 - Is This Claim Supported for This Use- - Trust and Assurance Calculus|B.3]] (Trust & Assurance, LOG‑use).
* `ev:` — **Evidence-account vocabulary**, used for source and support labels in a descriptive account. Each support or assurance claim retains its direct governing rule. *Anchor:* [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/10_Evidence Graph Referring- Claim-Bound Evidence and Provenance Graph/00_A.10 - Evidence Graph Referring- Claim-Bound Evidence and Provenance Graph|A.10]] / [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/02_03_Is This Claim Supported for This Use- - Trust and Assurance Calculus/00_B.03 - Is This Claim Supported for This Use- - Trust and Assurance Calculus|B.3]].
* `mero:` — **Mereology trace types** (internal labels: `SumTrace` / `SetTrace` / `SliceTrace`) used **informatively** in examples. *Anchor:* [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/00_01_Holon Aggregation and Part-Whole Construction/00_B.01 - Holon Aggregation and Part-Whole Construction|B.1]] (Γ‑aggregation).
