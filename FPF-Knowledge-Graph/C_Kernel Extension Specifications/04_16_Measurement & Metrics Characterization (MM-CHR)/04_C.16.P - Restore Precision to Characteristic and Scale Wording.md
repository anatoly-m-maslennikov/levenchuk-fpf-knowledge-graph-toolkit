5. **Repair hidden characteristic and scale construction.** When construction is hidden, recover the minimal needed set: characteristic, scale, value set, score, unit, scoring method, indicated characteristic or claim, exact direct indicator or proxy relation, comparison reference or comparator set, threshold rule or reference, admissible use, and any necessary applicability or stop condition. Add an explanatory non-admissible-use guard only under the full [[F_The Unification Suite (U-Suite)- Concept Sets, SenseCells, and System-Role Kinds and Assignments/19_Ontology-First Plain Technical Rewriting/00_F.19 - Ontology-First Plain Technical Rewriting|F.19]]:4 test. ## [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/04_16_Measurement & Metrics Characterization (MM-CHR)/04_C.16.P - Restore Precision to Characteristic and Scale Wording|C.16.P]]:12 - Consequences
