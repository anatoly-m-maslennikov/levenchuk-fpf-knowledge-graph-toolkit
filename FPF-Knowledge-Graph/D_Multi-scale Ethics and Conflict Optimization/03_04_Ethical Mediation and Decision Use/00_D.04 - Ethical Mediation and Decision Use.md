> **Type:** D-family ethical mediation and decision-use pattern
> **Status:** Stable
> **Pattern role:** This compact pattern contains the defining content for the ethical use of an already mapped conflict: mediation, refusal, evidence demand, bounded decision use, and residual handling.

**Use this when.** Use this pattern when an `InterlevelEthicalConflictDescription` from [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/02_03_Interlevel Ethical Conflict Structure/00_D.03 - Interlevel Ethical Conflict Structure|D.3]] must support mediation, refusal, a decision, an evidence demand, or a return to causal, assurance, or architecture work.

**Not this pattern when.** If the conflict has not yet been described, use [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/02_03_Interlevel Ethical Conflict Structure/00_D.03 - Interlevel Ethical Conflict Structure|D.3]]. If the issue is only value plurality, use [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/00_01_Ethical Value Plurality and FPF Boundary/00_D.01 - Ethical Value Plurality and FPF Boundary|D.1]]. If the issue is only entry recognition, use [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/01_02_Multilevel Ethics For Holon Work/00_D.02 - Multilevel Ethics For Holon Work|D.2]]. If the current work is bias, fairness, impact audit, causal-fairness audit consumption, or ethical assurance, use [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/04_05_Bias Audit and Ethical Assurance/00_D.05 - Bias Audit and Ethical Assurance|D.5]].

**What goes wrong if missed.** A mapped ethical conflict is treated as solved, blocked, or decision-ready without naming mediation, refusal, evidence demand, return, accepted residual, or bounded decision use.

**What this buys.** The practitioner can use one exact [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/02_03_Interlevel Ethical Conflict Structure/00_D.03 - Interlevel Ethical Conflict Structure|D.3]] conflict-description episteme for an admissible mediation action or bounded decision use while keeping evidence, causality, assurance, architecture, and bias-audit claims with their subject patterns.

## [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/03_04_Ethical Mediation and Decision Use/00_D.04 - Ethical Mediation and Decision Use|D.4]]:1 - Problem Frame

Once an interlevel ethical conflict is visible, the next risk is premature closure. A team may declare a compromise before evidence is sufficient, turn an assurance input into ethical permission, use one level's value as a trump card, or hide a refusal behind technical language.

[[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/03_04_Ethical Mediation and Decision Use/00_D.04 - Ethical Mediation and Decision Use|D.4]] guides one use of the conflict described by [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/02_03_Interlevel Ethical Conflict Structure/00_D.03 - Interlevel Ethical Conflict Structure|D.3]]. It does not make FPF a final moral authority. It asks what move is admissible from the current description and what must return to evidence, causality, assurance, architecture, decision, or value framing before action is justified.

## [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/03_04_Ethical Mediation and Decision Use/00_D.04 - Ethical Mediation and Decision Use|D.4]]:1.0 - Problem

A described ethical conflict can still be used badly. The failure is to treat its description, an assurance input, a formula, or an architecture return as if it already selected a compromise, refusal, evidence demand, accepted residual, or bounded decision use.

The record names the current ethical use of the conflict: mediate, refuse, continue under explicit residual, demand evidence, ask a causal question, ask for assurance, return to architecture, or make a bounded decision.

Name the affected EntityOfConcern and any affected Systems, the value-frame editions, the decision question and options, and the intended decision or Work use. Add ClaimScope and a qualification window when they delimit that use. State the proposed mediation or refusal and any accepted residuals. If evidence, causal adequacy, assurance, architecture residuals, responsibility, permission, or actual Work remains unresolved, return only that question to the pattern that defines it. These values delimit the mediation; a generic context field does not.

## [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/03_04_Ethical Mediation and Decision Use/00_D.04 - Ethical Mediation and Decision Use|D.4]]:4 - Archetypal Grounding (Worked Slices)

**Fair-share case.** A service outage plan can protect hospitals, households, or industrial customers, but not all at once. The [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/02_03_Interlevel Ethical Conflict Structure/00_D.03 - Interlevel Ethical Conflict Structure|D.3]] conflict description connects each affected scope and value concern to its consequence and horizon. [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/03_04_Ethical Mediation and Decision Use/00_D.04 - Ethical Mediation and Decision Use|D.4]] records the mediation use: options, accepted residuals, evidence demand, admitted decision Systems, prospective assignment requirements or commitments, direct decision-responsibility relations or exact missing governors, and return conditions. No assignment or Work is asserted merely because the plan names intended action. If execution occurs, the record adds the complete Work row. A mathematical allocation Method may have a separate [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/17_29_Mathematical Lens Use/00_C.29 - Mathematical Lens Use|C.29]] representation or lens-use assertion, but the allocation formula is not the ethical decision, assignment, or responsibility relation.

**Override case.** An assurance review says a release has the required technical assurance relation, but the [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/02_03_Interlevel Ethical Conflict Structure/00_D.03 - Interlevel Ethical Conflict Structure|D.3]] description shows unresolved harm for a subgroup. [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/03_04_Ethical Mediation and Decision Use/00_D.04 - Ethical Mediation and Decision Use|D.4]] does not let assurance override that conflict. It records whether release is refused, conditioned, delayed for evidence, handled under [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/16_28_CausalUse-CAL- Causal-Use Questions, Identification, and Realizability/00_C.28 - CausalUse-CAL- Causal-Use Questions, Identification, and Realizability|C.28]] causal-use analysis, or allowed with an explicit residual and an independently obtaining responsibility relation or exact missing governor.

[[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/03_04_Ethical Mediation and Decision Use/00_D.04 - Ethical Mediation and Decision Use|D.4]] does not define the conflict description, bias audit, ethical assurance, architecture residual, causal identification, evidence provenance, or decision theory in general. Do not name a mediation move "calculus" unless a mathematical lens is selected and the lens is actually doing work. Do not name a mediation move "operator" unless the current pattern explicitly governs an operation. Most [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/03_04_Ethical Mediation and Decision Use/00_D.04 - Ethical Mediation and Decision Use|D.4]] use is a bounded decision-use record, not a mathematical object.

## [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/03_04_Ethical Mediation and Decision Use/00_D.04 - Ethical Mediation and Decision Use|D.4]]:7 - Consequences

This pattern makes ethical action reviewable without pretending that every conflict has a clean optimum. It preserves refusal, evidence demand, and residual acceptance as first-class outcomes. It also prevents architecture, assurance, or causal evidence from quietly becoming moral permission.

## [[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/03_04_Ethical Mediation and Decision Use/00_D.04 - Ethical Mediation and Decision Use|D.4]]:9 - Rationale

[[D_Multi-scale Ethics and Conflict Optimization/03_04_Ethical Mediation and Decision Use/00_D.04 - Ethical Mediation and Decision Use|D.4]] exists because an inspectable ethical conflict still needs a bounded use. Some uses stop work. Some demand evidence. Some return to causal, assurance, or architecture patterns. Some proceed under an accepted residual with named responsibility and return conditions. Without this pattern, teams either freeze because conflict exists or move too fast because the conflict was mapped once.

The pattern keeps refusal, evidence demand, and residual acceptance visible as ordinary outcomes. It also prevents formulas, assurance labels, architecture residual repairs, or causal claims from silently becoming moral authorization.
