## [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]]:0 - Use This When

Use this pattern when exact capability, functioning, or transformation-flow facts, already established under their subject patterns, make a [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] whole-reidentification question live.

The first useful question is whether those facts can still be explained by the existing whole. If they can, keep that whole and use the direct pattern for the capability, functioning relation, transformation, Method, Work, module, characteristic, or architecture claim. If they cannot, use [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] for the residual question. Evidence and measurement separately support, challenge, or leave unresolved the claims about those facts; they create neither the facts nor [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] selection.

**What goes wrong if missed.** A genuine new whole is hidden under ordinary capability improvement; or every impressive capability, function, method chain, module allocation, or metric gain is overclaimed as emergence.

**What this buys.** The pattern keeps capability and functioning facts available to [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] while preserving the separate evidence relation and preventing [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]] from becoming a generic capability, function, method, work, module, or emergence pattern.

**Not this pattern when.**

- If the claim is ordinary capability, use [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/02_System-Role Kinds and Assignments/02_A.02.02 - System Capability- Conditions, Measures and Fit|A.2.2]]; use [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/04_16_Measurement & Metrics Characterization (MM-CHR)/00_C.16 - Measurement & Metrics Characterization (MM-CHR)|C.16]] for its measured-characteristic claims.
- If function-like wording hides the exact claim, use [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/06_Signature Stack- Classify and Place Boundary Claims/14_A.06.F - Function and Functional Precision Restoration (RPR-FUNCTION)|A.6.F]] to recover its direct subject pattern; an already precise functioning claim returns directly to that pattern.
- If the claim is transformation or transformation-flow structure, use [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/03_Transformer Quartet- Distinguish Work, Performer, Method and Method Description/04_U.Transformation- Actual Bounded Change Under Conditions/00_A.03.04 - U.Transformation- Actual Bounded Change Under Conditions|A.3.4]] or [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/17_18_Transformation Flow Structure/00_E.18 - Transformation Flow Structure|E.18]] respectively; use [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/18_30_Grounded Architecture and Selected-Structure Adequacy/07_C.30.TFS-REL - Use Transformation-Flow Structures and Networks in Architecture|C.30.TFS-REL]] for the selected structure's bounded architecture use.
- If the claim is method, method relation, method description, work plan, or work occurrence, use [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/15_System-Role-Method-Work Alignment/00_A.15 - System-Role-Method-Work Alignment|A.15]], [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/03_Transformer Quartet- Distinguish Work, Performer, Method and Method Description/01_U.Method- Reusable Way of Doing with Explicit Applicability/00_A.03.01 - U.Method- Reusable Way of Doing with Explicit Applicability|A.3.1]], [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/03_Transformer Quartet- Distinguish Work, Performer, Method and Method Description/02_U.MethodDescription- Description Episteme for a Way of Doing/00_A.03.02 - U.MethodDescription- Description Episteme for a Way of Doing|A.3.2]], [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/15_System-Role-Method-Work Alignment/02_A.15.02 - U.WorkPlan- Plan Content for Coordinating Future Work|A.15.2]], and [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/15_System-Role-Method-Work Alignment/01_A.15.01 - U.Work- Dated Performed Work Occurrence|A.15.1]].
- If bearer allocation is the question, use the direct allocation or parthood pattern. Use [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/06_Signature Stack- Classify and Place Boundary Claims/15_A.06.M - Module and Interface Claim Repair|A.6.M]] only for unresolved module/interface wording, and [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/18_30_Grounded Architecture and Selected-Structure Adequacy/00_C.30 - Grounded Architecture and Selected-Structure Adequacy|C.30]], [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/22_Structure and Structural Views (STRUCT-CAL)/00_A.22 - Structure and Structural Views (STRUCT-CAL)|A.22]] or [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/18_30_Grounded Architecture and Selected-Structure Adequacy/04_C.30.ASV - Architecture Structural View Adequacy (ASV)|C.30.ASV]] only for the current architecture, selected-structure or architectural-view claim.

## [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]]:1 - Problem Frame

A new capability is not automatically a new whole. A function-like relation is not automatically a part-whole relation. A transformation-flow structure is not automatically MHT.

[[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]] is the narrow [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] specialization for cases where exact capability, functioning, or transformation-flow facts defeat the existing-whole explanation and point to a candidate new holon. Evidence bears on claims about those facts; it does not make the explanation fail by itself.

## [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]]:2 - Problem

Without this specialization:

1. **Capability becomes generic emergence.** A threshold crossing or new envelope is treated as a new whole without [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] checks.
2. **Function becomes ontology.** Function-like wording creates `U.Function` or a hidden peer kind.
3. **Method and work collapse.** The way of doing, description of doing, planned work, and performed work are compressed into one vague operational claim.
4. **Module allocation becomes functional truth.** A module label is treated as evidence for the required behavior or selected structure.
5. **Transformation-flow description replaces in-life structure.** A graph, diagram, or publication of a flow is treated as the flow structure or whole.
6. **Whole reidentification is missed.** A real result whole is left as "just a better capability".

Subject-pattern explanations that often stop [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]] include:

- better measurement or benchmark normalization;
- improved component capability;
- corrected function-like wording;
- a clearer method relation or method family selection;
- a new method description without corresponding in-life capability or work facts;
- better work coordination inside the same whole;
- module allocation repair;
- architecture-view or transformation-flow-structure repair;
- better evidence, measurement, or source currentness for an unchanged world-side claim.

If one of these explanations is sufficient, finish [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]] by retaining the existing whole and continue with the subject pattern.

- a candidate episteme has exact constitution and explanatory-use facts that leave an episteme whole-reidentification question.

### [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]]:5.1 - Production Cell Capability

A milling machine, robot arm, fixture, and inspection station can remain a collection of assets even when a scheduling rule for their use is available. A new production-cell candidate becomes current only when exact capability, functioning, coordination, and transformation-flow facts cannot be explained by any single component or the old aggregate. Cell-level cycle time and tolerance measurements may support the claim; they do not create the candidate or those facts.

Use [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/06_Signature Stack- Classify and Place Boundary Claims/14_A.06.F - Function and Functional Precision Restoration (RPR-FUNCTION)|A.6.F]] for function-like wording, [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/03_Transformer Quartet- Distinguish Work, Performer, Method and Method Description/04_U.Transformation- Actual Bounded Change Under Conditions/00_A.03.04 - U.Transformation- Actual Bounded Change Under Conditions|A.3.4]] for transformations, [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/17_18_Transformation Flow Structure/00_E.18 - Transformation Flow Structure|E.18]] for transformation-flow structure and [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/18_30_Grounded Architecture and Selected-Structure Adequacy/07_C.30.TFS-REL - Use Transformation-Flow Structures and Networks in Architecture|C.30.TFS-REL]] for its bounded architecture use, [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/15_System-Role-Method-Work Alignment/01_A.15.01 - U.Work- Dated Performed Work Occurrence|A.15.1]] for performed work, [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/04_16_Measurement & Metrics Characterization (MM-CHR)/00_C.16 - Measurement & Metrics Characterization (MM-CHR)|C.16]] for cycle-time and tolerance characteristics, and [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] only when the cell whole must be reidentified.

### [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]]:5.2 - CI/CD Capability

A team may have methods for coding, testing, and releasing. That does not by itself create a new whole. Use the direct Method and Work patterns for the method relations and performed release work.

[[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]] becomes current only if exact capability, coordination, commitment, and work facts leave a result-holon question that the existing team or platform cannot explain. Evidence may support that claim; an automated delivery label or score does not decide the ontology.

### [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]]:5.3 - Theory Explains New Phenomena

A new theory may explain phenomena that the source portfolio did not explain. Use [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]] to bring the exact explanatory-use facts into [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]]'s ordinary existing-whole comparison, while evidence separately supports or challenges the claim about that use. This does not assign an [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/02_System-Role Kinds and Assignments/02_A.02.02 - System Capability- Conditions, Measures and Fit|A.2.2]] capability to the episteme. [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/03_B.02.03 - Meta-Holon Transition With Episteme Result|B.2.3]] supplies the episteme-result specialization if the exact candidate is `U.Episteme`; [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/00_02_Epistemic holon composition (KD-CAL)/01_C.02.01 - U.Episteme- Constitution, Empirical Grounding, and Edition Relations|C.2.1]] defines its constitution; [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/17_29_Mathematical Lens Use/00_C.29 - Mathematical Lens Use|C.29]] handles any mathematical-lens use.

## [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]]:8 - Consequences

Positive consequences:

- Exact capability and functioning facts can make real whole reidentification current, while evidence bears only on the associated claims.
- Subject patterns remain visible, so local improvements are not overclaimed.
- Method, work, function, module, and architecture distinctions survive high-pressure capability language; each claim remains with its subject pattern.

Costs:

- Teams must do the subject-pattern test before using [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]].
- Many impressive capability claims will stay outside MHT.
- [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]] depends on [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] for the whole-reidentification result and any durable record required by the receiving use.

## [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]]:9 - Rationale

Capabilities and functioning relations are often where a new-whole question first becomes visible. Their direct facts, not the availability of supporting evidence, determine whether the existing-whole explanation still works.

[[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Do Capability or Functioning Changes Require Whole Reidentification-|B.2.4]] keeps this mixed situation disciplined. It does not rename the capability, functioning, transformation flow, method, work, allocation, measurement, or support as "meta-function". It asks whether the exact direct facts defeat the existing-whole explanation and, only then, requires [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] for the residual question.
