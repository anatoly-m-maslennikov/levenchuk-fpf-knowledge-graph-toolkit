## [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]]:0 - Use This When

Use this pattern when exact capability, functioning, or transformation-flow facts, already established under their subject patterns, make a [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] whole-reidentification question live.

The first useful question is whether those facts can still be explained by the existing whole. If they can, keep that whole and use the direct pattern for the capability, functioning relation, transformation, Method, Work, module, characteristic, or architecture claim. If they cannot, use [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] for the residual question. Evidence and measurement separately support, challenge, or leave unresolved the claims about those facts; they create neither the facts nor [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] selection.

**What goes wrong if missed.** A genuine new whole is hidden under ordinary capability improvement; or every impressive capability, function, method chain, module allocation, or metric gain is overclaimed as emergence.

**What this buys.** The pattern keeps capability and functioning facts available to [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] while preserving the separate evidence relation and preventing [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]] from becoming a generic capability, function, method, work, module, or emergence pattern.

**Not this pattern when.**

- If the claim is ordinary capability, use [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/02_System-Role Kinds and Assignments/02_A.02.02 - U.Capability - System Ability Envelope and Measures|A.2.2]] and [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/04_16_Measurement & Metrics Characterization (MM-CHR)/00_C.16 - Measurement & Metrics Characterization (MM-CHR)|C.16]].
- If the claim is function-like wording or functioning relation without whole reidentification, use [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/06_Signature Stack & Boundary Discipline/14_A.06.F - Function and Functional Precision Restoration (RPR-FUNCTION)|A.6.F]].
- If the claim is transformation or transformation-flow structure, use [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/03_Transformer Constitution (Quartet)/04_U.Transformation- Bounded Change Under Conditions/00_A.03.04 - U.Transformation- Bounded Change Under Conditions|A.3.4]], [[E_The FPF Constitution and Authoring Guides/17_18_Transformation Flow Structure/00_E.18 - Transformation Flow Structure|E.18]], and [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/18_30_Grounded Architecture and Selected-Structure Adequacy/07_C.30.TFS-REL - Architecture Transformation-Flow Structure Relation|C.30.TFS-REL]].
- If the claim is method, method relation, method description, work plan, or work occurrence, use [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/15_System-Role-Method-Work Alignment/00_A.15 - System-Role-Method-Work Alignment|A.15]], [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/03_Transformer Constitution (Quartet)/01_A.03.01 - U.Method- Reusable Way of Doing with Explicit Applicability|A.3.1]], [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/03_Transformer Constitution (Quartet)/02_A.03.02 - U.MethodDescription- Description Episteme for a Way of Doing|A.3.2]], [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/15_System-Role-Method-Work Alignment/02_A.15.02 - U.WorkPlan|A.15.2]], and [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/15_System-Role-Method-Work Alignment/01_A.15.01 - U.Work|A.15.1]].
- If the claim is module allocation or bearer allocation, use [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/06_Signature Stack & Boundary Discipline/15_A.06.M - Module Relation Repair|A.6.M]], [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/18_30_Grounded Architecture and Selected-Structure Adequacy/00_C.30 - Grounded Architecture and Selected-Structure Adequacy|C.30]], [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/22_Structure and Structural Views (STRUCT-CAL)/00_A.22 - Structure and Structural Views (STRUCT-CAL)|A.22]], and [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/18_30_Grounded Architecture and Selected-Structure Adequacy/04_C.30.ASV - Architecture Structural View Adequacy (ASV)|C.30.ASV]] according to whether the live claim concerns the module, architecture, selected structure, or architectural structural view.
- If the claim is measurement, threshold, score, robustness, quality, or a whole-level characteristic, use [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/04_16_Measurement & Metrics Characterization (MM-CHR)/00_C.16 - Measurement & Metrics Characterization (MM-CHR)|C.16]] and [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/19_CharacteristicSpace & Dynamics Hook (A.CHR-SPACE)/00_A.19 - CharacteristicSpace & Dynamics Hook (A.CHR-SPACE)|A.19]] for that claim and [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/10_Evidence Graph Referring- Claim-Bound Evidence and Provenance Graph/00_A.10 - Evidence Graph Referring- Claim-Bound Evidence and Provenance Graph|A.10]] only when evidence use is current.
- If the wording is ambiguous emergence, synergy, or title-mnemonic language, use [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/01_B.02.P - Emergence and MHT Precision Restoration|B.2.P]] before selecting [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]].

## [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]]:1 - Problem Frame

A new capability is not automatically a new whole. A function-like relation is not automatically a part-whole relation. A transformation-flow structure is not automatically MHT.

[[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]] is the narrow [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] specialization for cases where exact capability, functioning, or transformation-flow facts defeat the existing-whole explanation and point to a candidate new holon. Evidence bears on claims about those facts; it does not make the explanation fail by itself.

## [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]]:2 - Problem

Without this specialization:

1. **Capability becomes generic emergence.** A threshold crossing or new envelope is treated as a new whole without [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] checks.
2. **Function becomes ontology.** Function-like wording creates `U.Function` or a hidden peer kind.
3. **Method and work collapse.** The way of doing, description of doing, planned work, and performed work are compressed into one vague operational claim.
4. **Module allocation becomes functional truth.** A module label is treated as evidence for the required behavior or selected structure.
5. **Transformation-flow description replaces in-life structure.** A graph, diagram, or publication of a flow is treated as the flow structure or whole.
6. **Whole reidentification is missed.** A real result whole is left as "just a better capability".

### [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]]:4.1 - Start From Exact Facts, Claims, And Support

1. Name the exact existing whole and its direct identity or reidentification rule.
2. Identify each exact capability envelope, obtaining functioning relation, or selected in-life transformation-flow structure under its subject pattern. Keep method, method description, work plan, work occurrence, module allocation, characteristic, and architecture facts separate when they are current.
3. State the exact claim made about those facts. Name evidence or measurement only as a separate relation that supports, challenges, or leaves that claim unresolved.
4. Perform [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]]'s ordinary existing-whole comparison. Better measurement, component improvement, Method or Work repair, allocation repair, or architecture-view repair can leave the same whole in place.
5. If a residual whole-reidentification question remains, use [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] for it. [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] then identifies one exact candidate new whole, applies the complete [[A_Kernel Architecture Cluster/01_Holon Ontic Foundation (U.Holon and Admitted Holon Kinds)/00_A.01 - Holon Ontic Foundation (U.Holon and Admitted Holon Kinds)|A.1]] and kind-specific criteria, and compares that candidate with the existing whole.

[[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]] adds no result species. If a receiving use needs a durable account, use [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]]'s optional [[C_Kernel Extension Specifications/00_02_Epistemic holon composition (KD-CAL)/01_C.02.01 - U.Episteme- Constitution, Empirical Grounding, and Edition Relations|C.2.1]] epistemes; their content neither creates the direct facts nor selects the new whole.

### [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]]:4.3 - Existing-Whole Explanation

Perform [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]]'s ordinary existing-whole comparison before claiming whole reidentification. Materialize its optional `ExistingWholeExplanationResult` only when another use must inspect or cite the outcome.

Subject-pattern explanations that often stop [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]] include:

- better measurement or benchmark normalization;
- improved component capability;
- corrected function-like wording;
- a clearer method relation or method family selection;
- a new method description without corresponding in-life capability or work facts;
- better work coordination inside the same whole;
- module allocation repair;
- architecture-view or transformation-flow-structure repair;
- better evidence, measurement, or source currentness for an unchanged world-side claim.

If one of these explanations is sufficient, do not use [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]]. Use the subject pattern.

### [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]]:4.4 - When [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]] Requires [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]]

Examples:

- a production cell has an exact capability envelope, obtaining coordination and functioning relations, a selected in-life transformation-flow structure, and external commitments that cannot be explained by individual machines or the old aggregate;
- a service platform has an obtaining functioning relation and external commitments that cannot be assigned to one service or module;
- a team, toolchain, and method family participate in exact coordination and work facts that make a result-system candidate live; or
- a candidate episteme has exact constitution and explanatory-use facts that leave an episteme whole-reidentification question.

After the return, use [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] for the existing-whole/new-whole comparison, its one exact candidate, and any optional record. The direct patterns still define and test the contributing facts, while evidence or measurement supports the associated claims.

A team may have methods for coding, testing, and releasing. That does not by itself create a new whole. Use the direct Method and Work patterns for the method relations and performed release work.

Evidence may support that claim; an automated delivery label or score does not decide the ontology.

## [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]]:8 - Consequences

Positive consequences:

- Exact capability and functioning facts can make real whole reidentification current, while evidence bears only on the associated claims.
- Subject patterns remain visible, so local improvements are not overclaimed.
- Method, work, function, module, and architecture distinctions survive high-pressure capability language; each claim remains with its subject pattern.

Costs:

- Teams must do the subject-pattern test before using [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]].
- Many impressive capability claims will stay outside MHT.
- [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]] depends on [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] for the final whole-reidentification record.

## [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]]:9 - Rationale

Capabilities and functioning relations are often where a new-whole question first becomes visible. Their direct facts, not the availability of supporting evidence, determine whether the existing-whole explanation still works.

[[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/04_B.02.04 - Capability and Functioning Whole Reidentification|B.2.4]] keeps this mixed situation disciplined. It does not rename the capability, functioning, transformation flow, method, work, allocation, measurement, or support as "meta-function". It asks whether the exact direct facts defeat the existing-whole explanation and, only then, requires [[B_Trans-disciplinary Reasoning Cluster/01_02_Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification/00_B.02 - Meta-Holon Transition - Whole Reidentification|B.2]] for the residual question.
