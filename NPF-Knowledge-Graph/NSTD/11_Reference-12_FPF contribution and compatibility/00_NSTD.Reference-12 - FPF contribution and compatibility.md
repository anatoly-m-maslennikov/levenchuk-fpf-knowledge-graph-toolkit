The definitions and general methods used by this edition are available in [FPF Core](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/fb3b6a4ad1d51d732a365f75f7d68337c7a751e2/FPF-Spec.md). In particular, `A.6.3.NAR` supplies the source-to-narrative relation and its ordinary and claim-specific uses; `C.2.8` supplies reader-qualified structural recovery; `A.19.ECS` and `C.16` supply the evaluation distinctions used in [[NSTD/20_06_Declared-Use Narrative Rendering Quality Evaluation/00_NSTD.06 - Declared-Use Narrative Rendering Quality Evaluation|NSTD.6]]. The support maps and the relations below identify the additional content used for each relevant claim.

NSTD depends on those definitions for the uses stated here. A relevant change calls for reconsidering the affected narrative instruction or evaluation; it does not require restarting unrelated uses. Compatibility across a changed Core edition is a separate question about that use. `E.4.PFR` explains this dependency distinction and `G.11` the currentness question.
